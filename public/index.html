About

DPM Tech Group is led by David P. Morris Jr., an enterprise and solutions architect with more than 18 years of experience delivering large-scale systems for federal agencies at Lockheed Martin and Leidos.

David authored reference architecture patterns for the Social Security Administration that supported its OneTech initiative and were implemented across CIO components such as disability and benefits. He has also supported the Federal Highway Administration's surface transportation and Cooperative Driving Automation research programs.

He speaks regularly on software engineering, with talks at IDUG and WeAreDevelopers on Db2, batch processing, and testing React backends.
